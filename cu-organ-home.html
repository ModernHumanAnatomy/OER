About the Collection

Advancing Anatomy
Through Preservation

Plastinated specimens are permanent anatomical preparations produced by replacing body fluids and fat with silicone or epoxy resins, resulting in odorless, durable, and highly detailed specimens. The Stabio Lab collection at CU Anschutz features 85 specimens spanning five major organ systems.

These specimens are integrated into the Modern Human Anatomy Master's program curriculum — including Neuroanatomy and the Advanced Imaging and Modeling (AIM) Track — supporting hands-on anatomical study, pathological comparison, and direct correlation with medical imaging modalities such as CT, MRI, and ultrasound.
