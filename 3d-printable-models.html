Nervous System

Brain Coronal Cross Sections

CU Modern Human Anatomy Program 3D scan from plastinated specimen

A 3D model created from a CT scan of a human brain from the CU Anschutz Plastination Library. Explore the brain in coronal cross-section, revealing internal structures including the ventricles, basal ganglia, and white matter tracts. Derived from a body donor through the State Anatomical Board of Colorado.

Cardiovascular

Heart Four Chambers

CU Modern Human Anatomy Program 3D scan + digital sculpting

Created from a combination of 3D-scanned plastinated specimens and digitally sculpted elements, this model is designed to reveal all four chambers of the heart in an opened view. Ideal for understanding the internal anatomy of the right and left atria and ventricles, the interventricular septum, and valvular anatomy.

Nervous System

Brainstem Nuclei with Cerebral Vasculature

CU Modern Human Anatomy Program 3D digital model

Depicts cranial nerve nuclei III–XII overlaid with cerebral vasculature, including the Edinger-Westphal nucleus, nucleus ambiguus, salivatory nuclei, dorsal motor nucleus of vagus, and nucleus solitarius. Developed specifically for stroke localization education — map vascular territory to expected cranial nerve deficits.

Respiratory

Larynx

Zachary Stetter Digital sculpting (no scan)

A fully digitally sculpted model of the larynx — no physical scanning was performed. The model is separated into two halves for internal exploration, revealing the thyroid cartilage, cricoid cartilage, arytenoid cartilages, vocal folds, and epiglottis. Useful for laryngoscopy preparation and phonation studies.
