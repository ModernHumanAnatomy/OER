We extend our deepest gratitude to the generous donors.

Brainstem Nuclei with Cerebral Vasculature

CU Modern Human Anatomy Program 3D digital model

Depicts cranial nerve nuclei III–XII overlaid with cerebral vasculature, including the Edinger-Westphal nucleus, nucleus ambiguus, salivatory nuclei, dorsal motor nucleus of vagus, and nucleus solitarius. Developed specifically for stroke localization education — map vascular territory to expected cranial nerve deficits.

Larynx

Zachary Stetter Digital sculpting (no scan)

A fully digitally sculpted model of the larynx — no physical scanning was performed. The model is separated into two halves for internal exploration, revealing the thyroid cartilage, cricoid cartilage, arytenoid cartilages, vocal folds, and epiglottis. Useful for laryngoscopy preparation and phonation studies.
