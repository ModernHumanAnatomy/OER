Our Mission

Anatomy Education,
Open to All

The Modern Human Anatomy Open Educational Resources initiative is a commitment to making high-quality anatomical teaching materials freely available to educators, students, and researchers under Creative Commons licensing.

Developed within the MHA Master's Program at the University of Colorado Anschutz Medical Campus, these resources reflect the program's integration of traditional gross anatomy with advanced computational tools — including 3D Slicer, MATLAB, TotalSegmentator, Cellpose, and MONAI.

Open Access

All resources are released under Creative Commons BY-NC-SA 4.0, freely available for educational and research use.

Multi-Modal

Physical specimens, 3D prints, imaging datasets, and digital models — covering the full spectrum of modern anatomy tools.

Curriculum-Aligned

Resources are developed in direct support of MHA program coursework, including neuroanatomy and the AIM Track.

Peer-Developed

Created and reviewed by faculty and students of the MHA Program, ensuring anatomical accuracy and pedagogical relevance.
